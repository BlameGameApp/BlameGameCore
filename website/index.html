    How to play

    1. Start a game

      One person starts a game and reads out the code. Everyone else types it in.

    2. Add photos

      The app picks five random photos from your camera roll. Reshuffle until you're happy, then share them.

    3. Blame someone

      Each round shows one photo for ten seconds. Tap who you think took it. The faster you're right, the more points you get.

    Hardcore mode

    Your photos are picked at random and shared without showing them to you first. The host turns it on, and the lobby tells everyone before they add a photo.

    What happens to your photos

    • You see them first

      Nothing leaves your phone until you share it. Hardcore mode is the one exception.

    • No location data

      Location and camera details are removed from every photo before it's stored.

    • Deleted after the party

      A room and its photos are deleted after six hours without activity.

    • No account

      You play with a name and a code. There's nothing to sign up for.
